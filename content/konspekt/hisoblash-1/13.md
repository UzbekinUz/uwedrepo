Metro bekatiga yo'lovchilar oqimi har soatda har xil: ertalab ko'p, tushda kam. Oqim tezligi f(t) (kishi/soat) ma'lum bo'lsa, kun davomida jami nechta yo'lovchi o'tganini qanday topamiz? ## Riman yig'indisidan integralgacha

[a; b] ni n bo'lakka bo'lamiz, har bo'lakdan c_{i} nuqta olamiz va to'g'ri to'rtburchaklar yuzalarini qo'shamiz:

$$
S_{n} = f(c_{1})Δx_{1} + f(c_{2})Δx_{2} + … + f(c_{n})Δx_{n}
$$

::: grafik x: -0.1..1.15, y: -0.1..1.15 | y = x² ostidagi yuza: 4 ta to'rtburchak (o'ng uchlar) bilan yaqinlashish, S₄ = 0,469
y = x^2 | teal | y = x²
soha 0.0625 | 0..0.25 | amber | Riman yig'indisi
soha 0.25 | 0.25..0.5 | amber |
soha 0.5625 | 0.5..0.75 | amber |
soha 1 | 0.75..1 | amber |
:::

::: tushuncha Ta'rif
Bo'laklar cheksiz maydalashganda (max Δx_{i} → 0) S_{n} ning limiti **aniq integral**:
$$
∫_{a}^{b} f(x)dx = lim_{max Δx_{i}→0} S_{n}
$$
:::

::: oddiy
f(x) ≥ 0 bo'lsa, ∫_{a}^{b} f(x)dx — grafik ostidagi **yuza**. Ox o'qidan pastdagi qism «−» ishora bilan qo'shiladi. Aniqmas integraldan farqi: natija — **son**.
:::

## Xossalar

::: formula
$$
∫_{a}^{a} f dx = 0,   ∫_{a}^{b} f dx = −∫_{b}^{a} f dx,   ∫_{a}^{b} f dx = ∫_{a}^{c} f dx + ∫_{c}^{b} f dx
∫_{a}^{b} (αf + βg)dx = α∫_{a}^{b} f dx + β∫_{a}^{b} g dx
$$
:::

- **Toq** funksiya: ∫_{−a}^{a} f dx = 0. **Juft:** ∫_{−a}^{a} f dx = 2∫_{0}^{a} f dx.
- **O'rta qiymat teoremasi:** ∫_{a}^{b} f dx = f(c)(b − a) qaysidir c ∈ [a; b] uchun. **O'rtacha qiymat:** {1|b − a}∫_{a}^{b} f dx.

::: grafik x: -2.2..2.2, y: -6..6 | Toq funksiya y = x³ + 4x: chap va o'ng yuzalar o'zaro yo'qotadi ⇒ ∫ = 0
y = x^3 + 4x | teal | y = x³ + 4x
soha x^3 + 4x | -2..0 | rose | manfiy qism
soha x^3 + 4x | 0..2 | teal | musbat qism
:::

## Nyuton–Leybnits formulasi

::: formula Integral hisobning asosiy formulasi
$$
∫_{a}^{b} f(x)dx = F(b) − F(a) = F(x)|_{a}^{b}
$$
bunda F — f ning istalgan boshlang'ich funksiyasi.
:::

::: teorema Yuqori chegarasi o'zgaruvchi integral
$$
({d|dx})∫_{a}^{x} f(t)dt = f(x),   ({d|dx})∫_{a}^{g(x)} f(t)dt = f(g(x))·g′(x)
$$
:::

::: eslatma Almashtirishda chegaralar ham almashadi
u = g(x) bo'lsa, yangi chegaralar u = g(a) va u = g(b) — eski o'zgaruvchiga qaytish **shart emas**.
:::

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol Nyuton–Leybnits
∫_{0}^{2} (3x^2 + 1)dx
---
(x^3 + x)|_{0}^{2} = (8 + 2) − 0 = **10**.
:::

::: misol Jadval integrallari
a) ∫_{1}^{e} {dx|x}; b) ∫_{0}^{π} sin x dx; c) ∫_{0}^{π/2} cos x dx.
---
a) ln x|_{1}^{e} = **1**. b) −cos x|_{0}^{π} = 1 + 1 = **2**. c) sin x|_{0}^{π/2} = **1**.
:::

::: misol Almashtirish
∫_{0}^{1} x(x^2 + 1)^3 dx
---
u = x^2 + 1, du = 2x dx; x: 0 → 1 ⇒ u: 1 → 2.

$$
{1|2}∫_{1}^{2} u^3du = {1|2}·{u^4|4}|_{1}^{2} = {16 − 1|8} = {15|8}
$$
:::

::: misol Ildizli almashtirish
∫_{0}^{4} {dx|1 + √x}
---
t = √x, x = t^2, dx = 2t dt; t: 0 → 2.

$$
∫_{0}^{2} {2t|1 + t}dt = 2∫_{0}^{2} (1 − {1|1 + t})dt = 2(t − ln(1 + t))|_{0}^{2} = 4 − 2 ln 3 ≈ 1,80
$$
:::

::: misol Bo'laklab
a) ∫_{0}^{1} xe^x dx; b) ∫_{1}^{e} ln x dx.
---
a) (x − 1)e^x|_{0}^{1} = 0 − (−1) = **1**. b) (x ln x − x)|_{1}^{e} = 0 − (−1) = **1**.
:::

::: misol Ta'rif bo'yicha (Riman yig'indisi)
∫_{0}^{1} x^2 dx ni yig'indining limiti sifatida hisoblang.
---
Δx = {1|n}, c_{i} = {i|n}:

$$
S_{n} = ∑ ({i|n})^2·{1|n} = {1|n^3}·{n(n + 1)(2n + 1)|6} = {(n + 1)(2n + 1)|6n^2} → {1|3}
$$

n = 4 da S_{4} = {5·9|96} ≈ 0,469 (grafikdagi to'rtburchaklar), n → ∞ da {1|3} ≈ 0,333.
:::

::: misol O'rtacha qiymat
f(x) = x^2 ning [0; 3] dagi o'rtacha qiymati va c.
---
{1|3}∫_{0}^{3} x^2dx = {1|3}·9 = **3**; c^2 = 3 ⇒ **c = √3**.
:::

::: misol Toq funksiya
∫_{−2}^{2} (x^3 + 4x)dx
---
Toq funksiya, simmetrik chegaralar ⇒ **0** (grafikda qizil va yashil yuzalar teng).
:::

::: misol O'zgaruvchi chegara
a) ({d|dx})∫_{0}^{x} √(1 + t^2)dt; b) ({d|dx})∫_{1}^{x^2} ln t dt.
---
a) **√(1 + x^2)**. b) ln(x^2)·2x = **4x ln x**.
:::

::: misol Iqtisodiy: jami mahsulot
Ishchi unumdorligi f(t) = 100 + 20t − 3t^2 (dona/soat). Birinchi 5 soatda qancha mahsulot?
---
∫_{0}^{5} (100 + 20t − 3t^2)dt = (100t + 10t^2 − t^3)|_{0}^{5} = 500 + 250 − 125 = **625 dona**.
:::

::: xato
- **Almashtirgandan keyin chegaralarni eski holida qoldirish.** 3-misolda 0 va 1 emas, 1 va 2.
- **«+ C» ni aniq integralga yozish.** F(b) − F(a) da C qisqarib ketadi.
- **Integralni doim yuza deb hisoblash.** ∫_{0}^{2π} sin x dx = 0, lekin yuza 4 (14-mavzu).
:::
