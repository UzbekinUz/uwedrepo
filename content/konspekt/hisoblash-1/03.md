## Limitlar haqida asosiy teoremalar

Agar lim_{x→c} f(x) = L va lim_{x→c} g(x) = M bo'lsa:

| Qoida | Formula |
|---|---|
| Yig'indi va ayirma | lim (f ± g) = L ± M |
| O'zgarmasga ko'paytma | lim k·f = k·L |
| Ko'paytma | lim f·g = L·M |
| Bo'linma | lim {f|g} = {L|M}, M ≠ 0 |
| Daraja | lim f^{n} = L^{n} |
| Ildiz | lim ⁿ√f = ⁿ√L |

Natija: ko'phad va ratsional funksiyalar uchun (maxraj nolga aylanmasa) limit — funksiyaning shu nuqtadagi qiymati: lim_{x→c} P(x) = P(c).

**Siqib olish (ikki militsioner) teoremasi.** Agar c atrofida g(x) ≤ f(x) ≤ h(x) va lim g = lim h = L bo'lsa, lim f = L.

## Aniqmasliklarni ochish usullari

- **{0|0}, ko'phadlar:** surat va maxrajni ko'paytuvchilarga ajratib, (x − c) ga qisqartirish.
- **{0|0}, ildizlar:** qo'shma ifodaga ko'paytirish.
- **{∞|∞}, x → ∞:** surat va maxrajni x ning eng katta darajasiga bo'lish.
- **∞ − ∞:** qo'shma ifodaga ko'paytirish yoki umumiy maxrajga keltirish.
- **Trigonometrik {0|0}:** birinchi ajoyib limit.
- **1^{∞}:** ikkinchi ajoyib limit.

## Ajoyib limitlar

$$
lim_{x→0} {sin x|x} = 1   (birinchi ajoyib limit)
lim_{x→∞} (1 + {1|x})^x = lim_{x→0} (1 + x)^{{1|x}} = e   (ikkinchi ajoyib limit)
$$

Ulardan kelib chiqadigan muhim limitlar (x → 0 da):

$$
{tg x|x} → 1,   {1 − cos x|x^2} → {1|2},   {ln(1 + x)|x} → 1,   {e^{x} − 1|x} → 1,   {a^{x} − 1|x} → ln a,   {(1 + x)^{k} − 1|x} → k
$$

## Ishlangan misollar

> **1-misol.** lim_{x→2} (x^3 + 2x − 1).
>
> **Yechim.** Ko'phad uchun qiymatni qo'yamiz: 8 + 4 − 1 = **11**.

> **2-misol** ({0|0}, ko'paytuvchilarga ajratish). lim_{x→1} {x^2 + 3x − 4|x^2 − 1}.
>
> **Yechim.** x^2 + 3x − 4 = (x − 1)(x + 4), x^2 − 1 = (x − 1)(x + 1). Qisqartirgach:
> $$
> lim_{x→1} {x + 4|x + 1} = {5|2}
> $$

> **3-misol** ({0|0}, ildiz). lim_{x→0} {√(x + 4) − 2|x}.
>
> **Yechim.** Qo'shma ifoda √(x + 4) + 2 ga ko'paytiramiz:
> $$
> {(x + 4) − 4|x(√(x + 4) + 2)} = {1|√(x + 4) + 2} → {1|4}
> $$

> **4-misol** ({∞|∞}). lim_{x→∞} {3x^2 − 2x + 1|6x^2 + 5} va lim_{x→∞} {2x + 1|x^2 + 3}.
>
> **Yechim.** Darajalar teng — bosh koeffitsiyentlar nisbati: {3|6} = **{1|2}**. Ikkinchisida maxraj darajasi katta — **0**.

> **5-misol** (ajoyib limit). Hisoblang: a) lim_{x→0} {sin 5x|x}; b) lim_{x→0} {tg 3x|sin 2x}; c) lim_{x→0} {1 − cos 4x|x^2}.
>
> **Yechim.**
> - a) {sin 5x|x} = 5·{sin 5x|5x} → 5·1 = **5**.
> - b) {tg 3x|sin 2x} = {tg 3x|3x}·{2x|sin 2x}·{3x|2x} → 1·1·{3|2} = **{3|2}**.
> - c) 1 − cos 4x = 2 sin^2 2x, shuning uchun {2 sin^2 2x|x^2} = 2·4·({sin 2x|2x})^2 → **8**.

> **6-misol** (1^{∞}). Hisoblang: a) lim_{x→∞} (1 + {3|x})^x; b) lim_{x→0} (1 + 2x)^{{1|x}}; c) lim_{x→∞} ({x + 1|x − 2})^x.
>
> **Yechim.**
> - a) (1 + {3|x})^x = [(1 + {3|x})^{{x|3}}]^3 → **e^3**.
> - b) (1 + 2x)^{{1|x}} = [(1 + 2x)^{{1|2x}}]^2 → **e^2**.
> - c) {x + 1|x − 2} = 1 + {3|x − 2}, ko'rsatkich x = {x|x − 2}·(x − 2) va {x|x − 2} → 1, demak limit **e^3**.

> **7-misol** (siqib olish). lim_{x→0} x^2 sin {1|x}.
>
> **Yechim.** −1 ≤ sin {1|x} ≤ 1 bo'lgani uchun −x^2 ≤ x^2 sin {1|x} ≤ x^2. Chetki ifodalar 0 ga intiladi, demak limit **0**.

> **8-misol.** a) lim_{x→0} {e^{3x} − 1|x}; b) lim_{x→0} {ln(1 + 4x)|x}.
>
> **Yechim.** a) {e^{3x} − 1|3x}·3 → **3**. b) {ln(1 + 4x)|4x}·4 → **4**.

> **9-misol** (∞ − ∞). lim_{x→∞} (√(x^2 + x) − x).
>
> **Yechim.** {x|√(x^2 + x) + x} = {1|√(1 + 1/x) + 1} → **{1|2}**.

## Mustaqil ishlash uchun

1. lim_{x→3} {x^2 − 5x + 6|x^2 − 9}. **Javob:** {1|6}.
2. lim_{x→0} {√(1 + x) − √(1 − x)|x}. **Javob:** 1.
3. lim_{x→∞} {5x^3 − x|2x^3 + 7x^2}. **Javob:** {5|2}.
4. lim_{x→0} {sin 7x|sin 3x}. **Javob:** {7|3}.
5. lim_{x→∞} (1 − {2|x})^{3x}. **Javob:** e^{−6}.
6. lim_{x→0} {e^{2x} − e^{x}|x}. **Javob:** 1.
