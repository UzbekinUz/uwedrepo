## Differensial tushunchasi

y = f(x) funksiyaning orttirmasi Δy = f(x + Δx) − f(x) ni quyidagicha yozish mumkin:

$$
Δy = f′(x)·Δx + α·Δx,   bunda α → 0 (Δx → 0 da)
$$

Orttirmaning asosiy (Δx ga nisbatan chiziqli) qismi **funksiya differensiali** deyiladi:

$$
dy = f′(x)·dx
$$

(erkli o'zgaruvchi uchun dx = Δx). Shu sababli hosilani {dy|dx} ko'rinishida ham yozamiz.

- **Geometrik ma'nosi:** dy — x dan x + Δx ga o'tganda **urinma** ordinatasining orttirmasi; Δy esa grafik ordinatasining orttirmasi.
- Kichik Δx da **Δy ≈ dy**.

## Differensial qoidalari

$$
d(u ± v) = du ± dv,   d(uv) = v du + u dv,   d({u|v}) = {v du − u dv|v^2}
$$

**Shakl invariantligi:** y = f(u) bo'lsa, u erkli o'zgaruvchi bo'lsin yoki boshqa funksiya bo'lsin, baribir dy = f′(u) du.

**Yuqori tartibli differensiallar:** d^2y = f″(x) dx^2, …, d^{n}y = f^{(n)}(x) dx^{n}.

## Taqribiy hisoblash

$$
f(x_{0} + Δx) ≈ f(x_{0}) + f′(x_{0})·Δx
$$

Muhim xususiy hollar (kichik x da): √(1 + x) ≈ 1 + {x|2}, (1 + x)^{n} ≈ 1 + nx, e^{x} ≈ 1 + x, ln(1 + x) ≈ x, sin x ≈ x.

**Xatoliklarni baholash:** o'lchash xatosi dx bo'lsa, natijaning absolyut xatosi ≈ |dy|, nisbiy xatosi ≈ {∣dy∣|∣y∣}.

## Ishlangan misollar

> **1-misol.** y = x^3 − 2x uchun x = 2, Δx = 0,1 da Δy va dy ni toping.
>
> **Yechim.** dy = (3x^2 − 2)dx = 10·0,1 = **1,0**. Δy = f(2,1) − f(2) = (9,261 − 4,2) − (8 − 4) = **1,061**. Farq 0,061 — Δx^2 tartibidagi kichik miqdor.

> **2-misol.** Differensialni toping: a) y = ln(sin x); b) y = e^{x^2}; c) y = arctg 2x.
>
> **Yechim.** a) dy = {cos x|sin x} dx = ctg x dx. b) dy = 2x e^{x^2} dx. c) dy = {2|1 + 4x^2} dx.

> **3-misol.** √4,04 ni taqriban hisoblang.
>
> **Yechim.** f(x) = √x, x_{0} = 4, Δx = 0,04, f′(x) = {1|2√x}. √4,04 ≈ 2 + {0,04|4} = **2,01** (aniq: 2,00998…).

> **4-misol.** ∛27,3 ≈ ?
>
> **Yechim.** f(x) = ∛x, f′(x) = {1|3∛(x^2)}, x_{0} = 27: f′(27) = {1|27}. ∛27,3 ≈ 3 + {0,3|27} ≈ **3,0111** (aniq: 3,01107…).

> **5-misol.** sin 31° ≈ ?
>
> **Yechim.** x_{0} = 30° = {π|6}, Δx = 1° = {π|180} ≈ 0,017453. sin 31° ≈ sin 30° + cos 30°·Δx = 0,5 + 0,8660·0,017453 ≈ **0,5151** (aniq: 0,51504…). Diqqat: Δx radianda olinadi!

> **6-misol.** a) 1,02^5; b) e^{0,05}.
>
> **Yechim.** a) (1 + x)^{n} ≈ 1 + nx: 1 + 5·0,02 = **1,1** (aniq: 1,10408…). b) e^{x} ≈ 1 + x: **1,05** (aniq: 1,05127…).

> **7-misol** (xatolik). Doira radiusi 10 sm, o'lchash xatosi 0,1 sm. Yuzaning xatosini baholang.
>
> **Yechim.** S = πr^2, dS = 2πr dr = 2π·10·0,1 = 2π ≈ **6,28 sm^2**. Nisbiy xato {dS|S} = {2dr|r} = **2%** (radius xatosi 1% bo'lsa, yuza xatosi 2 baravar katta).

> **8-misol** (ikkinchi tartibli differensial). y = x^4 uchun d^2y.
>
> **Yechim.** y″ = 12x^2, demak **d^2y = 12x^2 dx^2**.

> **9-misol** (iqtisodiy). C(x) = 0,1x^2 + 5x + 200. Ishlab chiqarish 50 dan 52 birlikka oshirilsa, xarajat taxminan qanchaga oshadi?
>
> **Yechim.** dC = C′(x)dx = (0,2x + 5)dx = (10 + 5)·2 = **30**. Aniq hisob: C(52) − C(50) = 730,4 − 700 = 30,4.
