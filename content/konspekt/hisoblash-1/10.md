::: maqsad
- Differensial dy va orttirma Δy farqi, geometrik ma'nosi
- Differensial qoidalari va shakl invariantligi
- Taqribiy hisoblash: √4,04, ∛27,3, sin 31° kalkulyatorsiz
- O'lchash xatolarini baholash va iqtisodiy taqribiy hisoblar
:::

::: hayot Hayotda qayerda kerak?
Xona tomoni 5 m deb o'lchandi, lekin ruletka 2 sm xato qilishi mumkin. Laminat sotib olayotganda maydon xatosi qancha bo'ladi? Ishlab chiqarishni 50 dan 52 birlikka oshirsak, xarajat taxminan qanchaga ko'payadi? Bunday savollarga murakkab formulani qayta hisoblamasdan, **differensial** — «urinma bo'yicha yaqinlashish» yordamida tez javob beriladi.
:::

## Differensial nima?

Funksiya orttirmasi Δy = f(x + Δx) − f(x) ikki qismdan iborat:

$$
Δy = f′(x)·Δx + (Δx ga nisbatan juda kichik qism)
$$

::: tushuncha Ta'rif
Orttirmaning asosiy, Δx ga chiziqli qismi — **differensial**:
$$
dy = f′(x)·dx
$$
(erkli o'zgaruvchi uchun dx = Δx). Shu sababli hosilani {dy|dx} deb ham yozamiz.
:::

::: oddiy
Δy — grafik bo'ylab haqiqiy o'zgarish; dy — **urinma** bo'ylab o'zgarish. Δx kichik bo'lsa, egri chiziq urinmadan deyarli farq qilmaydi, shuning uchun **Δy ≈ dy**.
:::

::: grafik x: 2..6.5, y: 1.2..2.7 | y = √x va x = 4 dagi urinma: kichik oraliqda deyarli bir xil
y = sqrt(x) | teal | y = √x
y = 2 + (x-4)/4 | amber | urinma y = 2 + (x − 4)/4
nuqta 4, 2 | (4; 2)
:::

## Qoidalar

::: formula
$$
d(u ± v) = du ± dv,   d(uv) = v du + u dv,   d({u|v}) = {v du − u dv|v^2}
d^2y = f″(x)dx^2,   d^n y = f^{(n)}(x)dx^n
$$
:::

::: teorema Shakl invariantligi
y = f(u) bo'lsa, u erkli o'zgaruvchi bo'ladimi yoki boshqa funksiyami — baribir **dy = f′(u)du**. Shuning uchun almashtirish usulida differensiallarni erkin almashtiramiz.
:::

## Taqribiy hisoblash

::: formula Asosiy formula
$$
f(x_{0} + Δx) ≈ f(x_{0}) + f′(x_{0})·Δx
$$
:::

| Kichik x da | Taqribiy formula |
|---|---|
| √(1 + x) | 1 + {x|2} |
| (1 + x)^n | 1 + nx |
| e^x | 1 + x |
| ln(1 + x) | x |
| sin x | x (radianda) |

::: eslatma x_{0} ni qanday tanlash kerak?
Berilgan songa eng yaqin, **hisoblash oson** sonni oling: √4,04 uchun 4; ∛27,3 uchun 27; sin 31° uchun 30°. Burchak orttirmasi **radianda**: 1° = {π|180} ≈ 0,01745.
:::

## Xatoliklarni baholash

O'lchash xatosi dx bo'lsa: absolyut xato ≈ ∣dy∣, nisbiy xato ≈ {∣dy∣|∣y∣}.

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol dy va Δy ni solishtirish
y = x^3 − 2x uchun x = 2, Δx = 0,1 da dy va Δy ni toping.
---
dy = (3x^2 − 2)dx = 10·0,1 = **1,0**.

Δy = f(2,1) − f(2) = (9,261 − 4,2) − (8 − 4) = **1,061**.

Farq atigi 0,061 — Δx^2 tartibidagi miqdor.
:::

::: misol Differensialni topish
a) y = ln(sin x); b) y = e^{x^2}; c) y = arctg 2x.
---
a) dy = {cos x|sin x}dx = ctg x dx. b) dy = 2xe^{x^2}dx. c) dy = {2|1 + 4x^2}dx.
:::

::: misol Kvadrat ildiz
√4,04 ≈ ?
---
f(x) = √x, x_{0} = 4, Δx = 0,04, f′(4) = {1|2√4} = {1|4}.

√4,04 ≈ 2 + {1|4}·0,04 = **2,01** (aniq: 2,00998…).
:::

::: misol Kub ildiz
∛27,3 ≈ ?
---
f′(x) = {1|3∛(x^2)}, f′(27) = {1|27}. ∛27,3 ≈ 3 + {0,3|27} ≈ **3,0111** (aniq: 3,01107…).
:::

::: misol Trigonometrik
sin 31° ≈ ?
---
x_{0} = 30°, Δx = 1° ≈ 0,017453 rad.

sin 31° ≈ sin 30° + cos 30°·Δx = 0,5 + 0,8660·0,017453 ≈ **0,5151** (aniq: 0,51504…).
:::

::: misol Daraja va ko'rsatkich
a) 1,02^5; b) e^{0,05}.
---
a) 1 + 5·0,02 = **1,1** (aniq: 1,10408…). b) 1 + 0,05 = **1,05** (aniq: 1,05127…).
:::

::: misol O'lchash xatosi
Doira radiusi 10 sm, xato 0,1 sm. Yuza xatosi?
---
S = πr^2, dS = 2πr dr = 2π·10·0,1 = 2π ≈ **6,28 sm²**.

Nisbiy xato: {dS|S} = {2dr|r} = **2%** — radiusdagi 1% xato yuzada 2 baravar kattalashadi.
:::

::: misol Iqtisodiy taqribiy hisob
C(x) = 0,1x^2 + 5x + 200. Ishlab chiqarish 50 dan 52 ga oshsa, xarajat taxminan qanchaga oshadi?
---
dC = C′(x)dx = (0,2·50 + 5)·2 = **30**. Aniq: C(52) − C(50) = 730,4 − 700 = 30,4.
:::

::: misol Ikkinchi tartibli differensial
y = x^4 uchun d^2y.
---
y″ = 12x^2 ⇒ **d^2y = 12x^2 dx^2**.
:::

::: xato
- **Burchakni gradusda qo'yish.** sin 31° ≈ 0,5 + 0,866·**1** — noto'g'ri; Δx = 0,01745 radian.
- **x_{0} ni noqulay tanlash.** √4,04 uchun x_{0} = 4,04 deb olsangiz, hech narsa yutmaysiz.
- **dy va Δy ni tenglashtirish.** Ular faqat **taqriban** teng, Δx kichik bo'lgandagina.
:::
