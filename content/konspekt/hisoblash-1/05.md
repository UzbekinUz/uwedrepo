## Hosila ta'rifi

f funksiyaning x nuqtadagi **hosilasi** — funksiya orttirmasining argument orttirmasiga nisbatining limiti:

$$
f′(x) = lim_{Δx→0} {Δy|Δx} = lim_{h→0} {f(x + h) − f(x)|h}
$$

- **Geometrik ma'nosi:** f′(x_{0}) — grafikka (x_{0}; f(x_{0})) nuqtada o'tkazilgan urinmaning burchak koeffitsiyenti.
- **Fizik (iqtisodiy) ma'nosi:** o'zgarishning **oniy tezligi** (masalan, yo'lning hosilasi — tezlik, xarajatning hosilasi — marjinal xarajat).
- Hosilasi mavjud funksiya **differensiallanuvchi** deyiladi; differensiallanuvchi funksiya uzluksiz (teskarisi doim to'g'ri emas: |x| funksiya 0 da uzluksiz, lekin hosilasi yo'q).

**Urinma tenglamasi:**

$$
y − f(x_{0}) = f′(x_{0})·(x − x_{0})
$$

## Differensiallash qoidalari

$$
(u ± v)′ = u′ ± v′,   (c·u)′ = c·u′
(u·v)′ = u′v + uv′,   ({u|v})′ = {u′v − uv′|v^2}
$$

**Murakkab funksiya hosilasi** (zanjir qoidasi): y = f(u), u = g(x) bo'lsa, y′ = f′(u)·u′.

## Hosilalar jadvali

| f(x) | f′(x) | f(x) | f′(x) |
|---|---|---|---|
| c (o'zgarmas) | 0 | sin x | cos x |
| x^{n} | n·x^{n−1} | cos x | −sin x |
| √x | {1|2√x} | tg x | {1|cos^2 x} |
| {1|x} | −{1|x^2} | ctg x | −{1|sin^2 x} |
| e^{x} | e^{x} | arcsin x | {1|√(1 − x^2)} |
| a^{x} | a^{x} ln a | arccos x | −{1|√(1 − x^2)} |
| ln x | {1|x} | arctg x | {1|1 + x^2} |
| log_{a} x | {1|x ln a} | arcctg x | −{1|1 + x^2} |

## Maxsus hollar

- **Parametrik funksiya** x = x(t), y = y(t): y′_{x} = {y′_{t}|x′_{t}}.
- **Oshkormas funksiya** F(x, y) = 0: tenglikning ikki tomonini x bo'yicha differensiallab (y ni x ning funksiyasi deb), y′ ni topamiz.
- **Teskari funksiya:** x′_{y} = {1|y′_{x}}.
- **Logarifmik differensiallash:** (ln y)′ = {y′|y} ⇒ y′ = y·(ln y)′. y = u^{v} ko'rinishidagi funksiyalar uchun qulay.
- **Yuqori tartibli hosilalar:** y″ = (y′)′, y‴ = (y″)′, …

## Ishlangan misollar

> **1-misol** (ta'rif bo'yicha). f(x) = x^2 + 3x ning hosilasini toping.
>
> **Yechim.**
> $$
> {f(x + h) − f(x)|h} = {(x + h)^2 + 3(x + h) − x^2 − 3x|h} = {2xh + h^2 + 3h|h} = 2x + h + 3
> $$
> h → 0 da **f′(x) = 2x + 3**.

> **2-misol** (ta'rif bo'yicha). f(x) = √x.
>
> **Yechim.** {√(x + h) − √x|h} ni qo'shma ifodaga ko'paytiramiz: {(x + h) − x|h(√(x + h) + √x)} = {1|√(x + h) + √x} → **{1|2√x}**.

> **3-misol.** y = 3x^4 − {2|x} + 5√x.
>
> **Yechim.** y′ = 12x^3 + {2|x^2} + {5|2√x}.

> **4-misol** (ko'paytma va bo'linma). a) y = x^2 e^{x}; b) y = {x^2 + 1|x − 1}.
>
> **Yechim.** a) y′ = 2x·e^{x} + x^2·e^{x} = **(x^2 + 2x)e^{x}**.
> b) y′ = {2x(x − 1) − (x^2 + 1)·1|(x − 1)^2} = **{x^2 − 2x − 1|(x − 1)^2}**.

> **5-misol** (zanjir qoidasi).
> - y = sin(3x^2 + 1) ⇒ y′ = cos(3x^2 + 1)·6x.
> - y = ln(x^2 + 4) ⇒ y′ = {2x|x^2 + 4}.
> - y = (2x − 5)^{10} ⇒ y′ = 10(2x − 5)^9·2 = 20(2x − 5)^9.
> - y = e^{−x^2} ⇒ y′ = −2x·e^{−x^2}.

> **6-misol** (urinma). y = x^3 − 2x egri chiziqqa x_{0} = 1 nuqtada urinma tenglamasini tuzing.
>
> **Yechim.** y(1) = −1, y′ = 3x^2 − 2, y′(1) = 1. Urinma: y + 1 = 1·(x − 1), ya'ni **y = x − 2**.

> **7-misol** (parametrik). x = t^2, y = t^3 − t. y′_{x} ni va t = 1 dagi qiymatini toping.
>
> **Yechim.** y′_{x} = {3t^2 − 1|2t}. t = 1 da {3 − 1|2} = **1**.

> **8-misol** (oshkormas). x^2 + y^2 = 25 aylanaga (3; 4) nuqtada urinma.
>
> **Yechim.** x bo'yicha differensiallaymiz: 2x + 2y·y′ = 0 ⇒ y′ = −{x|y}. (3; 4) da y′ = −{3|4}. Urinma: y − 4 = −{3|4}(x − 3), ya'ni **3x + 4y = 25**.

> **9-misol** (logarifmik). y = x^{x} (x > 0).
>
> **Yechim.** ln y = x ln x. Differensiallaymiz: {y′|y} = ln x + 1. Demak **y′ = x^{x}(ln x + 1)**.

> **10-misol** (teskari funksiya). y = x^3 + x. y = 2 nuqtada x′_{y} ni toping.
>
> **Yechim.** y = 2 da x = 1. y′_{x} = 3x^2 + 1 = 4, demak x′_{y} = **{1|4}**.

> **11-misol** (yuqori tartib). a) y = x^4 − 3x^2; b) y = e^{2x}.
>
> **Yechim.** a) y′ = 4x^3 − 6x, **y″ = 12x^2 − 6**. b) y′ = 2e^{2x}, y″ = 4e^{2x}, umuman **y^{(n)} = 2^{n} e^{2x}**.

## Mustaqil ishlash uchun

1. y = (x^2 − 3x)^5. **Javob:** 5(x^2 − 3x)^4(2x − 3).
2. y = {ln x|x}. **Javob:** {1 − ln x|x^2}.
3. y = x·sin x. **Javob:** sin x + x cos x.
4. y = √(1 + x^2) ning x = 0 dagi urinmasi. **Javob:** y = 1.
5. x^3 + y^3 = 2xy bo'lsa, y′ ni toping. **Javob:** y′ = {2y − 3x^2|3y^2 − 2x}.
6. y = ln(cos x) ning y″ si. **Javob:** −{1|cos^2 x}.
