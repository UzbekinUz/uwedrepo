::: maqsad
- Egri chiziqlar orasidagi yuzani hisoblash
- Aylanish jismi hajmi va yoy uzunligi
- Iqtisodiy tatbiqlar: xarajatni tiklash, jami mahsulot, iste'molchi va ishlab chiqaruvchi yutug'i, joriy qiymat
:::

::: hayot Hayotda qayerda kerak?
Bozorda tarvuzni 15 ming so'mga olishga tayyor edingiz, sotuvchi 10 mingga berdi — siz 5 ming so'm «yutdingiz». Butun bozor bo'yicha bunday yutuqlar yig'indisi — **iste'molchi yutug'i** — talab egri chizig'i va narx chizig'i orasidagi **yuza**. Davlat narxni cheklasa yoki soliq kiritsa, iqtisodchilar aynan shu yuzalar qanchaga o'zgarishini hisoblab, siyosatni baholaydi.
:::

## Yuza

::: formula Ikki egri chiziq orasidagi yuza
[a; b] da f(x) ≥ g(x) bo'lsa:
$$
S = ∫_{a}^{b} (f(x) − g(x))dx   — «yuqoridagi minus pastdagi»
$$
a va b — odatda f(x) = g(x) tenglamaning ildizlari (kesishish nuqtalari).
:::

::: eslatma Har doim chizmadan boshlang
Qaysi chiziq yuqorida ekanini rasmda ko'rasiz. f(x) ishorasi o'zgarsa, kesmani bo'laklarga bo'lib, manfiy qismni «−» bilan oling.
:::

::: grafik x: -1.5..3.5, y: -1.5..10 | y = x² va y = 2x + 3 orasidagi yuza: S = 32/3
y = x^2 | teal | y = x²
y = 2x + 3 | amber | y = 2x + 3
soha 2x + 3 ; x^2 | -1..3 | amber | S = 32/3
nuqta -1, 1 | (−1; 1)
nuqta 3, 9 | (3; 9)
:::

## Hajm va yoy uzunligi

::: formula
| Kattalik | Formula |
|---|---|
| Ox atrofida aylanish jismi hajmi | V = π∫_{a}^{b} f^2(x)dx |
| Ikki egri chiziq orasini aylantirish | V = π∫_{a}^{b} (f^2 − g^2)dx |
| Yoy uzunligi | L = ∫_{a}^{b} √(1 + (f′(x))^2)dx |
:::

::: oddiy Hajm formulasi qayerdan?
Jismni Ox ga perpendikulyar yupqa «tangalarga» kesamiz: har biri radiusi f(x), qalinligi dx bo'lgan silindr, hajmi πf^2(x)dx. Hammasini qo'shsak — integral.
:::

## Iqtisodiy tatbiqlar

| Masala | Formula |
|---|---|
| Xarajatni MC dan tiklash | C(x) = C(0) + ∫_{0}^{x} MC(t)dt |
| Hajm x_{1} dan x_{2} ga oshganda xarajat o'sishi | ∫_{x_{1}}^{x_{2}} MC dx |
| Jami mahsulot (unumdorlik f(t)) | Q = ∫_{0}^{T} f(t)dt |
| Iste'molchi yutug'i (talab p = D(x), muvozanat (x_{0}; p_{0})) | CS = ∫_{0}^{x_{0}} D(x)dx − p_{0}x_{0} |
| Ishlab chiqaruvchi yutug'i (taklif p = S(x)) | PS = p_{0}x_{0} − ∫_{0}^{x_{0}} S(x)dx |
| Daromad oqimining joriy qiymati | PV = ∫_{0}^{T} I(t)e^{−rt}dt |

::: grafik x: 0..4.2, y: 0..21 | Talab p = 20 − x², taklif p = 2x + 12: iste'molchi (yashil) va ishlab chiqaruvchi (sariq) yutug'i
y = 20 - x^2 | teal | talab p = 20 − x²
y = 2x + 12 | amber | taklif p = 2x + 12
y = 16 | slate | muvozanat narx p₀ = 16 | 0..2
soha 20 - x^2 ; 16 | 0..2 | teal | CS = 16/3
soha 16 ; 2x + 12 | 0..2 | amber | PS = 4
nuqta 2, 16 | muvozanat (2; 16)
:::

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol Ikki egri chiziq
y = x^2 va y = x orasidagi yuza.
---
Kesishish: x^2 = x ⇒ x = 0, 1. [0; 1] da x ≥ x^2:

$$
S = ∫_{0}^{1} (x − x^2)dx = ({x^2|2} − {x^3|3})|_{0}^{1} = {1|6}
$$
:::

::: misol Parabola va to'g'ri chiziq
y = x^2 va y = 2x + 3 orasidagi yuza.
---
x^2 = 2x + 3 ⇒ x = −1, 3. To'g'ri chiziq yuqorida:

$$
S = ∫_{−1}^{3} (2x + 3 − x^2)dx = (x^2 + 3x − {x^3|3})|_{−1}^{3} = 9 − (−{5|3}) = {32|3}
$$
:::

::: misol Ishorasi o'zgaradigan funksiya
y = sin x va Ox o'qi orasidagi yuza, [0; 2π].
---
[π; 2π] da sin x ≤ 0, shuning uchun:

S = ∫_{0}^{π} sin x dx − ∫_{π}^{2π} sin x dx = 2 + 2 = **4**.

(∫_{0}^{2π} sin x dx = 0 — bu yuza emas!)
:::

::: grafik x: -0.3..6.6, y: -1.3..1.3 | y = sin x: yuza 4, lekin integral 0
y = sin(x) | teal | y = sin x
soha sin(x) | 0..pi | teal | +2
soha sin(x) | pi..2pi | rose | −2
:::

::: misol Aylanish jismi
y = √x, 0 ≤ x ≤ 4 egri chiziq Ox atrofida aylantirildi. Hajm?
---
V = π∫_{0}^{4} x dx = π·{x^2|2}|_{0}^{4} = **8π** ≈ 25,1.
:::

::: misol Yoy uzunligi
y = {2|3}x^{3/2}, 0 ≤ x ≤ 3.
---
y′ = x^{1/2} ⇒ 1 + (y′)^2 = 1 + x.

$$
L = ∫_{0}^{3} √(1 + x)dx = {2|3}(1 + x)^{3/2}|_{0}^{3} = {2|3}(8 − 1) = {14|3}
$$
:::

::: misol Xarajat o'sishi
MC(x) = 3x^2 − 4x + 10. Ishlab chiqarish 2 dan 4 birlikka oshganda xarajat qanchaga ortadi?
---
∫_{2}^{4} (3x^2 − 4x + 10)dx = (x^3 − 2x^2 + 10x)|_{2}^{4} = 72 − 20 = **52**.
:::

::: misol Iste'molchi va ishlab chiqaruvchi yutug'i
Talab p = 20 − x^2, taklif p = 2x + 12.
---
Muvozanat: 20 − x^2 = 2x + 12 ⇒ x^2 + 2x − 8 = 0 ⇒ x_{0} = 2, p_{0} = 16.

- CS = ∫_{0}^{2} (20 − x^2)dx − 2·16 = (40 − {8|3}) − 32 = **{16|3} ≈ 5,33**.
- PS = 32 − ∫_{0}^{2} (2x + 12)dx = 32 − 28 = **4**.

(Grafikdagi yashil va sariq sohalar.)
:::

::: misol Joriy qiymat
Yiliga 1 000 $ uzluksiz daromad 5 yil davom etadi, stavka 5%. Joriy qiymati?
---
PV = ∫_{0}^{5} 1000e^{−0,05t}dt = {1000|0,05}(1 − e^{−0,25}) ≈ 20 000·0,2212 ≈ **4 424 $**.

Ya'ni bugungi 4 424 $ kelajakdagi 5 000 $ lik oqimga teng — pulning vaqt qiymati.
:::

::: xato
- **Yuzani topishda manfiy qismni qo'shib yuborish.** ∫_{0}^{2π} sin x dx = 0, yuza esa 4.
- **«Yuqori − pastki» tartibini almashtirish.** Natija manfiy chiqsa — tartib noto'g'ri.
- **Hajm formulasida kvadratni unutish.** V = π∫ f^2dx, π∫ f dx emas.
:::

## Mustaqil mashqlar

::: mashq
y = x^3 va y = x orasidagi to'liq yuza || {1|2}
y = e^x, 0 ≤ x ≤ 1 ni Ox atrofida aylantirish hajmi || {π|2}(e^2 − 1) ≈ 10,04
