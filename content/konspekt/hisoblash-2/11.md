## Modellashtirish qadamlari

1. **O'zgaruvchilarni aniqlash:** vaqt t, kattalik y(t).
2. **O'zgarish qonunini yozish:** «tezlik nimaga mutanosib?» ⇒ y' = f(t, y).
3. **Turini aniqlash va yechish** (ajraladigan, chiziqli, o'zgarmas koeffitsiyentli…).
4. **Talqin qilish:** muvozanat, barqarorlik, t → ∞ dagi xulq.

## Asosiy modellar

::: formula Tabiiy o'sish
y' = ky ⇒ y = y_{0}e^{kt}. Uzluksiz foiz, aholi, inflyatsiya (qisqa muddatda).
:::

::: formula Narxning muvozanatga qaytishi (Evans)
Talab D(p) = a − bp, taklif S(p) = c + dp. Narx ortiqcha talabga mutanosib o'zgaradi:

$$
p' = k(D − S) = k(a − c) − k(b + d)p
$$

Yechim: p(t) = p^{*} + (p_{0} − p^{*})e^{−k(b + d)t}, bu yerda **p^{*} = {a − c|b + d}** — muvozanat narx. k, b, d > 0 bo'lgani uchun p → p^{*}: bozor **barqaror**.
:::

::: formula Logistik o'sish
O'sish tezligi ham hozirgi qiymatga, ham «qolgan bo'sh joy»ga mutanosib:

$$
y' = ky(1 − {y|N}) ⇒ y = {N|1 + Ae^{−kt}}, A = {N − y_{0}|y_{0}}
$$

N — bozor sig'imi (to'yinish darajasi). Eng tez o'sish y = N/2 da.
:::

::: grafik x: 0..16, y: 0..1100 | Logistik egri chiziq: N = 1000, y(0) = 100, k = 0,5
y = 1000/(1 + 9exp(-0.5x)) | teal | y(t)
gorizontal 1000 | N = 1000 (bozor sig'imi)
gorizontal 500 | N/2
nuqta 4.394, 500 | eng tez o'sish (t ≈ 4,4)
:::

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol Evans modeli
D = 10 − p, S = 2 + p, k = 0,5, p(0) = 10. p(t) = ? p(2) = ?
---
Muvozanat: 10 − p = 2 + p ⇒ **p^{*} = 4**.

p' = 0,5(8 − 2p) = 4 − p ⇒ p' + p = 4 (chiziqli). Yechim: p = 4 + Ce^{−t}, p(0) = 10 ⇒ C = 6.

**p(t) = 4 + 6e^{−t}**; p(2) = 4 + 6·0,1353 ≈ **4,81**. Narx yuqoridan muvozanatga tushib boradi.
:::

::: misol Yangi mahsulot sotuvi
Shaharda 1000 ta potensial xaridor. Dastlab 100 kishi sotib olgan, k = 0,5 (oy^{−1}). Qachon xaridorlarning yarmi sotib oladi?
---
A = {1000 − 100|100} = 9: y(t) = {1000|1 + 9e^{−0,5t}}.

y = 500 ⇒ 1 + 9e^{−0,5t} = 2 ⇒ e^{−0,5t} = {1|9} ⇒ t = 2 ln 9 ≈ **4,4 oy**. Shu paytda sotuv eng tez o'sadi, keyin sekinlashadi — reklama byudjetini rejalashtirish uchun muhim nuqta.
:::

::: misol Elastiklikdan talab funksiyasi
Talabning narx bo'yicha elastikligi doim −2 ga teng, p = 5 da Q = 40. Q(p) = ?
---
{dQ|dp}·{p|Q} = −2 ⇒ {dQ|Q} = −2{dp|p} ⇒ ln Q = −2ln p + ln C ⇒ Q = Cp^{−2}.

40 = C/25 ⇒ C = 1000: **Q = {1000|p^2}**.
:::

::: misol Kutilmali bozor (2-tartibli)
Talab xaridorlarning narx o'zgarishi haqidagi kutilmasini hisobga oladi: D = 20 − p − 3p' − p'', S = −4 + p. p(t) qanday o'zgaradi?
---
D = S: −p'' − 3p' − 2p + 24 = 0 ⇒ **p'' + 3p' + 2p = 24**.

Xarakteristik: r^2 + 3r + 2 = 0 ⇒ r = −1; −2. Xususiy yechim p^{*} = 12 (o'zgarmas): 2·12 = 24 ✓.

**p = 12 + C_{1}e^{−t} + C_{2}e^{−2t}**. Ikkala ildiz manfiy — narx tebranmasdan muvozanat 12 ga yaqinlashadi.
:::

::: xato
- **Mutanosiblik koeffitsiyenti ishorasini xato olish.** Ortiqcha talab (D > S) narxni oshiradi — k > 0.
- **Logistik tenglamada A ni y_{0} deb olish.** A = (N − y_{0})/y_{0}.
- **Muvozanatni topib, barqarorlikni tekshirmaslik.** Ildizlarning ishorasi hal qiladi.
:::
