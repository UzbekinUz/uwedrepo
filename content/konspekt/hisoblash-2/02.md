## Xususiy hosilalar

z = f(x, y) ning x bo'yicha **xususiy hosilasi** — y ni o'zgarmas deb, faqat x bo'yicha olingan oddiy hosila:

$$
f'_{x} = {∂z|∂x} = lim_{Δx→0} {f(x + Δx, y) − f(x, y)|Δx}
$$

f'_{y} ham xuddi shunday — endi x o'zgarmas. Hisoblash 1 dagi barcha qoidalar (ko'paytma, bo'linma, murakkab funksiya) o'zgarmaydi; faqat «qaysi harf hozir son» ekanini unutmaslik kerak.

::: tushuncha Iqtisodiy ma'no — chekli (marginal) kattaliklar
Q = f(K, L) uchun:
- **MP_{L} = {∂Q|∂L}** — mehnatning chekli mahsuloti: L ni 1 birlikka oshirganda Q ning taxminiy o'sishi (K o'zgarmas).
- **MP_{K} = {∂Q|∂K}** — kapitalning chekli mahsuloti.
Foydalilik U(x, y) uchun {∂U|∂x} — x tovarning chekli foydaliligi.
:::

## Yuqori tartibli hosilalar

Xususiy hosilalardan yana hosila olsak, to'rtta ikkinchi tartibli hosila chiqadi: f''_{xx}, f''_{yy} va **aralash** f''_{xy}, f''_{yx}.

::: teorema Shvarts teoremasi
Agar aralash hosilalar uzluksiz bo'lsa, **f''_{xy} = f''_{yx}** — differensiallash tartibi ahamiyatsiz. Bu hisobni tekshirishning yaxshi usuli.
:::

## To'liq differensial

$$
dz = f'_{x}dx + f'_{y}dy
$$

dz — funksiya orttirmasining **chiziqli bosh qismi**. Kichik dx, dy uchun:

$$
f(x_{0} + Δx, y_{0} + Δy) ≈ f(x_{0}, y_{0}) + f'_{x}Δx + f'_{y}Δy
$$

## Gradient va yo'nalish bo'yicha hosila

**Gradient** — xususiy hosilalardan tuzilgan vektor: grad f = (f'_{x}; f'_{y}).

l yo'nalishning birlik vektori e = (cos α; cos β) bo'lsa, **yo'nalish bo'yicha hosila**:

$$
{∂f|∂l} = f'_{x}cos α + f'_{y}cos β = grad f · e
$$

::: teorema Gradientning asosiy xossalari
1. Funksiya **grad f yo'nalishida eng tez o'sadi**, eng katta tezlik ∣grad f∣ ga teng.
2. −grad f yo'nalishida eng tez kamayadi.
3. Gradient sath chizig'iga **perpendikulyar**; sath chizig'i bo'ylab hosila 0.
:::

::: grafik x: -3..3, y: -3..3 | f = x² + y²: (1; 2) dagi sath chizig'i (aylana), urinma va gradient yo'nalishi
y = sqrt(5 - x^2) | teal | sath chizig'i f = 5
y = -sqrt(5 - x^2) | teal |
y = (5 - x)/2 | slate | urinma x + 2y = 5 | -1..3
y = 2x | amber | grad f = (2; 4) yo'nalishi | 1..1.5
nuqta 1, 2 | (1; 2)
:::

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol Xususiy va aralash hosilalar
z = x^3y^2 + 2xy − 5y. Hamma birinchi va ikkinchi tartibli hosilalarni toping.
---
z'_{x} = 3x^2y^2 + 2y; z'_{y} = 2x^3y + 2x − 5.

z''_{xx} = 6xy^2; z''_{yy} = 2x^3.

z''_{xy} = (3x^2y^2 + 2y)'_{y} = 6x^2y + 2; z''_{yx} = (2x^3y + 2x − 5)'_{x} = 6x^2y + 2. **Teng** — Shvarts teoremasi bajarildi.
:::

::: misol Chekli mahsulot
Q = 4K^{0,5}L^{0,5}, K = 100, L = 25. MP_{K} va MP_{L}?
---
MP_{K} = 2K^{−0,5}L^{0,5} = 2·{5|10} = **1**.

MP_{L} = 2K^{0,5}L^{−0,5} = 2·{10|5} = **4**.

Bitta qo'shimcha ishchi ≈ 4 birlik, bitta qo'shimcha kapital birligi ≈ 1 birlik mahsulot beradi — hozircha mehnatga sarflash foydaliroq (narxlar teng bo'lsa).
:::

::: misol Differensial bilan taqribiy hisob
√(3,02^2 + 3,97^2) ni taqribiy hisoblang.
---
f(x, y) = √(x^2 + y^2), x_{0} = 3, y_{0} = 4: f = 5.

f'_{x} = {x|√(x^2 + y^2)} = {3|5}, f'_{y} = {4|5}; Δx = 0,02, Δy = −0,03.

f ≈ 5 + 0,6·0,02 + 0,8·(−0,03) = 5 + 0,012 − 0,024 = **4,988**. (Aniq qiymat 4,98811 — xato 0,0001 dan kam.)
:::

::: misol Gradient va yo'nalish bo'yicha hosila
f = x^2 + xy + y^2, M(1; 2). l = (3; 4) yo'nalishidagi hosila va eng katta o'sish tezligi?
---
grad f = (2x + y; x + 2y) = (4; 5) M nuqtada.

e = {1|5}(3; 4) (chunki ∣l∣ = 5). {∂f|∂l} = 4·{3|5} + 5·{4|5} = {32|5} = **6,4**.

Eng katta tezlik ∣grad f∣ = √(16 + 25) = **√41 ≈ 6,40** — (4; 5) yo'nalishida. l yo'nalishi gradientga juda yaqin, shuning uchun 6,4 deyarli maksimumga teng.
:::

::: xato
- **x bo'yicha hosilada y ni ham differensiallash.** f'_{x} da y — oddiy son: (x^2y)'_{x} = 2xy, (y^3)'_{x} = 0.
- **Yo'nalish vektorini birlik vektorga keltirmaslik.** l = (3; 4) ni avval 5 ga bo'lish kerak.
- **Gradientni son deb yozish.** grad f — vektor; uning uzunligi esa son.
:::
