## Yuza va hajm

$$
S = ∫∫_{D} dxdy, V = ∫∫_{D} f(x, y) dxdy (f ≥ 0)
$$

Ikki sirt orasidagi jism hajmi: V = ∫∫_{D}(f_{2} − f_{1})dxdy, bu yerda D — jismning Oxy tekislikdagi proyeksiyasi.

::: grafik x: -0.3..2.4, y: -0.5..4.5 | y = 2x va y = x² orasidagi soha (0 ≤ x ≤ 2)
y = 2x | teal | y = 2x
y = x^2 | amber | y = x²
soha 2x ; x^2 | 0..2 | teal | S = 4/3
nuqta 2, 4 | (2; 4)
:::

## O'rtacha qiymat

$$
f̄ = {1|S}∫∫_{D} f(x, y) dxdy
$$

— D sohadagi barcha nuqtalar bo'yicha «o'rtacha balandlik». Bir o'zgaruvchidagi {1|b − a}∫_{a}^{b} f dx ning umumlashmasi.

## Massa va og'irlik markazi

ρ(x, y) zichlikli plastinka uchun:

::: formula Formulalar
- Massa: **m = ∫∫_{D} ρ dxdy**
- Statik momentlar: M_{y} = ∫∫_{D} xρ dxdy, M_{x} = ∫∫_{D} yρ dxdy
- Og'irlik markazi: **x_{c} = {M_{y}|m}, y_{c} = {M_{x}|m}**
:::

Iqtisodiy talqin: ρ — aholi zichligi bo'lsa, m — jami aholi, (x_{c}; y_{c}) — xizmat ko'rsatish markazini joylashtirish uchun tabiiy nuqta.

## Ikki o'lchovli ehtimollik zichligi

(X, Y) tasodifiy juftlik zichligi f(x, y) ≥ 0 bo'lsa:

::: formula Asosiy tengliklar
- Normallash: ∫∫ f(x, y) dxdy = 1 (butun soha bo'yicha).
- Ehtimol: P((X, Y) ∈ G) = ∫∫_{G} f(x, y) dxdy.
- Kutilma: M(X) = ∫∫ x·f(x, y) dxdy.
:::

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol Yuza
y = 2x va y = x^2 bilan chegaralangan soha yuzasi.
---
Kesishish: x^2 = 2x ⇒ x = 0; 2. Oraliqda 2x ≥ x^2.

S = ∫_{0}^{2} dx ∫_{x^2}^{2x} dy = ∫_{0}^{2}(2x − x^2)dx = 4 − {8|3} = **{4|3}**.
:::

::: misol Hajm
z = 4 − x^2 − y^2 paraboloid va z = 0 tekislik orasidagi jism hajmi.
---
Proyeksiya: 4 − x^2 − y^2 ≥ 0 ⇒ doira x^2 + y^2 ≤ 4. Qutb koordinatalarida:

V = ∫_{0}^{2π} dφ ∫_{0}^{2}(4 − r^2)r dr = 2π·[2r^2 − {r^4|4}]_{0}^{2} = 2π(8 − 4) = **8π ≈ 25,1**.
:::

::: misol O'rtacha ishlab chiqarish
Q = 10√(KL), K va L 1 dan 4 gacha o'zgaradi. O'rtacha Q?
---
D = [1; 4] × [1; 4], S = 9. Integral ajraladi: ∫_{1}^{4}√K dK = {2|3}(8 − 1) = {14|3}.

∫∫_{D} 10√K√L dKdL = 10·{14|3}·{14|3} = {1960|9}.

Q̄ = {1|9}·{1960|9} = {1960|81} ≈ **24,2**. (Taqqoslang: o'rta nuqta K = L = 2,5 da Q = 25 — o'rtacha undan biroz kam, chunki √ botiq funksiya.)
:::

::: misol Og'irlik markazi
D = [0; 1] × [0; 2], zichlik ρ = x + y.
---
m = ∫_{0}^{1}dx∫_{0}^{2}(x + y)dy = ∫_{0}^{1}(2x + 2)dx = 1 + 2 = **3**.

M_{y} = ∫_{0}^{1}dx∫_{0}^{2}(x^2 + xy)dy = ∫_{0}^{1}(2x^2 + 2x)dx = {2|3} + 1 = {5|3} ⇒ x_{c} = {5|9}.

M_{x} = ∫_{0}^{1}dx∫_{0}^{2}(xy + y^2)dy = ∫_{0}^{1}(2x + {8|3})dx = 1 + {8|3} = {11|3} ⇒ y_{c} = {11|9}.

**(x_{c}; y_{c}) = ({5|9}; {11|9})** — geometrik markaz (0,5; 1) dan zichroq tomonga (o'ng-yuqoriga) siljigan.
:::

::: misol Ehtimollik
f(x, y) = c(x + y), 0 ≤ x, y ≤ 1. c = ? P(X + Y ≤ 1) = ?
---
∫_{0}^{1}∫_{0}^{1}(x + y)dxdy = {1|2} + {1|2} = 1 ⇒ **c = 1**.

P(X + Y ≤ 1) = ∫_{0}^{1}dx∫_{0}^{1 − x}(x + y)dy = ∫_{0}^{1}{1 − x^2|2}dx = {1|2}·{2|3} = **{1|3}**.
:::
