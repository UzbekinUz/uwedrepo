::: hayot Hayotda qayerda kerak?
Real modellarning aksariyati — masalan, y' = x^2 + y^2 — elementar funksiyalarda **yechilmaydi**. Excel, Python yoki makroiqtisodiy prognoz dasturlari bunday tenglamalarni qadam-baqadam, **sonli** yechadi. Bu mavzu ularning «ichida» nima borligini ko'rsatadi — natijaga qancha ishonish mumkinligini bilish uchun.
:::

## Masalaning qo'yilishi

Koshi masalasi: y' = f(x, y), y(x_{0}) = y_{0}. [x_{0}; b] oraliqni **h qadam** bilan bo'lamiz: x_{i} = x_{0} + ih. Maqsad — y(x_{i}) ning taqribiy qiymatlari y_{i} ni topish.

## Eyler usuli

Har bir nuqtada hosilani (urinma qiyaligini) bilamiz: y'(x_{i}) = f(x_{i}, y_{i}). Kichik qadamda funksiyani urinma bilan almashtiramiz:

$$
y_{i+1} = y_{i} + h·f(x_{i}, y_{i})
$$

Geometrik ma'no — **Eyler siniq chizig'i**: har qadamda urinma bo'ylab yuramiz. Xato har qadamda h^2 tartibda, butun oraliqda **h** tartibda (1-tartibli usul): qadamni 2 baravar kichraytirsak, xato ham ≈ 2 baravar kamayadi.

::: grafik x: 0..1.3, y: 0.9..2.9 | y' = y, y(0) = 1: aniq yechim eˣ va Eyler siniq chizig'i (h = 0,25)
y = exp(x) | teal | aniq y = eˣ
y = 1 + x | amber | 1-qadam | 0..0.25
y = 1.25 + 1.25(x - 0.25) | amber | 2-qadam | 0.25..0.5
y = 1.5625 + 1.5625(x - 0.5) | amber | 3-qadam | 0.5..0.75
y = 1.953125 + 1.953125(x - 0.75) | amber | 4-qadam | 0.75..1
nuqta 1, 2.4414 | Eyler: 2,441
nuqta 1, 2.71828 | e ≈ 2,718
:::

## Takomillashtirilgan Eyler (Xoyn) usuli

Bitta qiyalik o'rniga qadam boshidagi va (Eyler bilan taxmin qilingan) oxiridagi qiyaliklarning **o'rtachasi** olinadi:

$$
k_{1} = f(x_{i}, y_{i}), k_{2} = f(x_{i} + h, y_{i} + hk_{1}), y_{i+1} = y_{i} + {h|2}(k_{1} + k_{2})
$$

Bu **2-tartibli** usul: h ni 2 baravar kichraytirsak, xato ≈ 4 baravar kamayadi.

## Runge–Kutta usuli (4-tartibli)

::: formula RK4
$$
k_{1} = f(x_{i}, y_{i}), k_{2} = f(x_{i} + {h|2}, y_{i} + {h|2}k_{1})
$$
$$
k_{3} = f(x_{i} + {h|2}, y_{i} + {h|2}k_{2}), k_{4} = f(x_{i} + h, y_{i} + hk_{3})
$$
$$
y_{i+1} = y_{i} + {h|6}(k_{1} + 2k_{2} + 2k_{3} + k_{4})
$$
Qiyaliklarning tortilgan o'rtachasi (Simpson formulasiga o'xshash). Xato h^{4} tartibda — amaliyotdagi «standart» usul.
:::

## Ishlangan misollar

::: misol Eyler usuli
y' = x + y, y(0) = 1, h = 0,1. y(0,3) ni toping.
---
f(x, y) = x + y.

y_{1} = 1 + 0,1·(0 + 1) = 1,1.

y_{2} = 1,1 + 0,1·(0,1 + 1,1) = 1,22.

y_{3} = 1,22 + 0,1·(0,2 + 1,22) = **1,362**.
:::

::: misol Xoyn usulining birinchi qadami
Shu masala, x_{0} = 0 dan x_{1} = 0,1 ga.
---
k_{1} = 0 + 1 = 1; Eyler taxmini 1 + 0,1·1 = 1,1; k_{2} = f(0,1; 1,1) = 1,2.

y_{1} = 1 + 0,05·(1 + 1,2) = **1,11**.
:::

::: misol RK4 ning birinchi qadami
Shu masala, h = 0,1.
---
k_{1} = f(0; 1) = 1.

k_{2} = f(0,05; 1 + 0,05·1) = 0,05 + 1,05 = 1,1.

k_{3} = f(0,05; 1 + 0,05·1,1) = 0,05 + 1,055 = 1,105.

k_{4} = f(0,1; 1 + 0,1·1,105) = 0,1 + 1,1105 = 1,2105.

y_{1} = 1 + {0,1|6}(1 + 2,2 + 2,21 + 1,2105) = 1 + {0,1|6}·6,6205 ≈ **1,110342**.
:::

::: misol Usullarni taqqoslash
Aniq yechim y = 2e^{x} − x − 1 (8-mavzu usuli bilan topiladi). Natijalar:
---
| x | Eyler | Xoyn | RK4 | Aniq |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0,1 | 1,1 | 1,11 | 1,1103417 | 1,1103418 |
| 0,2 | 1,22 | 1,24205 | 1,2428051 | 1,2428055 |
| 0,3 | 1,362 | 1,398465 | 1,3997170 | 1,3997176 |

x = 0,3 dagi xato: Eyler ≈ 0,038, Xoyn ≈ 0,0013, RK4 ≈ 0,0000006. Bir xil qadamda RK4 Eylerdan **60 000 marta** aniqroq.
:::

::: xato
- **f(x_{i}, y_{i}) da x_{i} ni yangilashni unutish** — x ham har qadamda h ga oshadi.
- **RK4 da k_{2}, k_{3} ni h emas, h/2 bilan hisoblash kerakligini unutish.**
- **Katta qadam olib, natijaga ishonish.** Eyler y' = y uchun h = 0,25 da e o'rniga 2,44 beradi (10% xato).
:::
